Il Primo Tower penthouse


Contractor — Private
Interior Desginer  — WEDO interiors

Occupying one of the highest residences in Downtown Dubai, this ultra-luxury penthouse at Il Primo Tower
demanded a furniture collection equal to the scale, exclusivity, and prestige of its setting.

Calibro was entrusted with designing and manufacturing a complete portfolio of 
bespoke furniture, created exclusively for this residence. Every piece was developed 
to maximize the expansive interiors while delivering exceptional 
comfort, refined proportions, and uncompromising craftsmanship.
The result is a residence defined by individuality rather than repetition: a fully bespoke 
furniture collection that transforms one of Dubai's most prestigious penthouses
into a home of enduring elegance, exceptional quality, and timeless luxury.

Emerald Hills, Dubai Hills


Contractor — Private
Interior Design — jadex Studio
Furniture — Custom-made by Calibro
Location — Emerald Hills, Dubai Hills
Size — 1715.8 sqm



For this private villa in Emerald Hills, Calibro partnered with jadex Studio to deliver a fully bespoke furniture
 collection that complements the architectural vision from concept through completion. Every piece was
 engineered exclusively for this residence, ensuring consistency in proportion, material 
selection, detailing, and functionality throughout the home.

From the main living areas to the private suites, the furniture was developed to respond to the scale
 of each space rather than adapt to standard dimensions. Premium materials, precision manufacturing, 
and meticulous finishing define every element, creating a collection that is both 
visually refined and built for long-term performance.

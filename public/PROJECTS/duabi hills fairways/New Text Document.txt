Dubai Hills Fairways



Contractor — Private
Interior Design — jadex Studio
Furniture — Custom-made by Calibro
Location — Dubai Hills Fairways, Dubai
Size — 1,502 sqm



Nestled within the prestigious Fairways community at Dubai Hills Estate, this newly completed ultra-luxury villa 
represents a seamless union of architectural vision, bespoke craftsmanship, and timeless design. Conceived from
 the ground up as a fully integrated interior, every space was designed to express a singular identity rather than follow
 trends or decorative conventions.

The project is the result of a close collaboration between Jadex Studio, who led the interior design
 vision, and Calibro, whose artisans transformed every concept into handcrafted furniture made exclusively
 for this residence. Rather than selecting from existing collections, every furnishing was individually designed,
 engineered, and manufactured specifically for the home—ensuring perfect harmony 
between architecture, proportion, and lifestyle.

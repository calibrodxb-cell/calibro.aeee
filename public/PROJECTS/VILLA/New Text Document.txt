Study House 

Contractor — Private
Interior Design — ISTO ARCHITECTURE 
Furniture — Custom-made by Calibro
Location —Wadi Al Amardi, Al Khawaneej
Size — 203.9  smq



Created for a private study house in Wadi Al Amardi, Al Khawaneej, this project centers around a bespoke 
cinema room where comfort, craftsmanship, and performance come together in perfect balance.
At the heart of the space is a collection of Calibro's custom-made recliner sofas, 
designed exclusively for this residence. Engineered for extended viewing sessions, 
each recliner combines ergonomic support with luxurious upholstery, precision reclining 
mechanisms, and meticulously crafted detailing, delivering an exceptional level of 
comfort without compromising on aesthetics.
The result is a home entertainment experience that reflects 
Calibro's commitment to precision manufacturing, timeless design, and uncompromising luxury.

SO/Uptown Tower


Contractor — Private
Furniture — Custom-made by Calibro
Location — SO/Uptown Tower
Size — 314.6 sqm sqm



The secondary reception at SO/Uptown Tower was conceived as an extension of the building's contemporary 
identity—where every furnishing contributes to a refined and memorable arrival experience.

Working closely with the project team, Calibro delivered a fully bespoke furniture package, developing 
custom pieces tailored specifically to the spatial layout, circulation, and design intent of the reception area. 
Every element was engineered to integrate seamlessly with the interior architecture while meeting 
the demands of a high-traffic hospitality environment.
